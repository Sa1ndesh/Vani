# Legal Data Sources

All legal data in `ai-service/data/` is based on publicly available Indian legislation.

## Sources

- **IPC / BNS**: India Code portal (indiacode.nic.in)
- **Tenancy laws**: Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs model act text
- **Family law**: Hindu Marriage Act 1955, Special Marriage Act 1954, Hindu Succession Act 1956
- **Property law**: Transfer of Property Act 1882, Registration Act 1908, RERA 2016
- **State laws**: Respective state government gazette notifications
- **Dialect data**: Academic linguistic surveys and community input

## Disclaimer

This data is for informational purposes only and does not constitute legal advice.
Always consult a qualified lawyer for specific legal matters.
